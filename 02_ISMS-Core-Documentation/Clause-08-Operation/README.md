# Clause 8 — Operation

This folder contains documentation related to the operational planning and control of the ISMS and the implementation of information security risk treatment processes.

## Coverage

- Operational planning and control
- Information security risk assessment activities
- Information security risk treatment activities
- Implementation and maintenance of risk treatment plans
- Management of planned operational changes
- Control of externally provided processes where applicable

## Purpose

To demonstrate how ISMS requirements and risk treatment activities are implemented, controlled and maintained during day-to-day operations.
