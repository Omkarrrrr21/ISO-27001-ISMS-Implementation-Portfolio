# Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation

This folder contains documentation related to monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation and management review of the ISMS.

## Coverage

- Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation
- Information security performance indicators
- Internal audit
- Internal audit programme and planning
- Audit results and findings
- Management review
- Management review inputs and outputs
- Evaluation of ISMS performance and effectiveness

## Purpose

To demonstrate how the organization evaluates the performance and effectiveness of the ISMS through monitoring, measurement, internal audit and management review.
