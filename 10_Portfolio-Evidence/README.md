# Portfolio Evidence

This folder contains selected, sanitized portfolio evidence used to demonstrate the practical implementation and documentation capabilities presented in this repository.

## Evidence Categories

- Risk Management
- Statement of Applicability
- Access Management
- Asset Management
- Incident Management
- Security Awareness
- Business Continuity
- Disaster Recovery
- Internal Audit
- Continual Improvement

## Evidence Selection

Only selected evidence should be presented here to provide recruiters and reviewers with a concise view of the overall project.

Detailed source documents and registers are maintained in their respective repository sections.

## Security & Privacy

No passwords, credentials, confidential organizational information, personal data or production security information should be included in the repository.

## Evidence Classification

Materials in this section are portfolio / demonstration evidence unless explicitly identified otherwise.

## Purpose

To provide a recruiter-friendly view of practical GRC and ISMS implementation work without exposing unnecessary or sensitive project information.
