# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

This folder contains the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery documentation and supporting evidence developed as part of the ISMS portfolio.

## Coverage

- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
- Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
- Recovery / DR Testing
- Business Continuity Exercises
- BC/DR Review and Continual Improvement

## BC/DR Lifecycle

**BIA → BCP → DRP → Recovery Testing → Business Continuity Exercise → Review & Continual Improvement**

## Purpose

To demonstrate a structured approach for identifying business continuity requirements, defining recovery strategies, testing recovery arrangements and incorporating lessons learned into continual improvement.

## Evidence Classification

BC/DR registers and supporting records in this portfolio are presented as portfolio / demonstration evidence unless explicitly identified otherwise.
